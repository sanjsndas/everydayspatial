About

Spaces matter.
We write about why.

PLINTH & PLEAT is an independent journal on interior design, architecture, and the quiet intelligence of everyday spaces. We publish long-form essays, practical guides, and critical reflections — no trend-chasing, no noise.

Est. 2019 Independent & reader-supported Ad-free
Our Mission

To make spatial thinking accessible — and beautiful.

We believe that good design is not a luxury, but a language. A well-proportioned room, a thoughtfully placed window, a material that ages with grace — these are not trivial details. They shape how we feel, how we move, and how we live.

Our mission is to bridge the gap between professional practice and everyday curiosity. We translate architectural ideas into clear, useful prose — without dumbing them down, and without mystifying them.

Every article is written by practising architects, designers, and researchers who care deeply about the built environment. We take the long view: we are more interested in what lasts than what is trending.

Editorial Principles

What we stand for.

  • 01
    Depth over speed We publish fewer articles, but each one is researched, considered, and built to last.
  • 02
    Practical poetry We write about ideas — but always with an eye to how they translate into real rooms and real lives.
  • 03
    Independence We are reader-supported. We do not accept sponsored content, and we do not chase clicks.
  • 04
    Sustainability as standard We believe the most sustainable building is the one that already exists — and the next best is built to last.
  • 05
    Human scale Architecture is for people. We never lose sight of the body, the senses, and the rituals of daily life.

How we work

Slow journalism for the built environment.

Each article begins with a question — not a keyword. We research deeply, speak to practitioners, and test ideas against real projects. We do not publish listicles or surface-level trend reports.

We work with a small group of contributing architects, designers, and historians. Every piece is edited collaboratively, and we fact-check carefully. When we get something wrong, we correct it transparently.

Our editorial calendar is driven by curiosity, not by commercial pressures. We publish when a piece is ready — not to fill a schedule.
