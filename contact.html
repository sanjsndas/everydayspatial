Pitch guidelines

We welcome pitches from writers, architects, and designers. Please include:

  • A working title and 2–3 paragraph outline
  • Why this story matters now
  • A short writing sample or link

Send to pitch@plinthandpleat.com

Before you write

We accept a limited number of well-researched guest essays. We do not publish sponsored posts or advertorials. Any commercial partnership is clearly labelled and kept separate from editorial content.

We aim to reply to all messages within two working days. Pitches may take slightly longer if they require editorial review. If you haven't heard back within a week, feel free to follow up.

We occasionally allow republishing of shorter excerpts with clear attribution and a link back to the original article. Full republishing requires written permission. Contact editor@plinthandpleat.com.

We take accuracy seriously. Please email editor@plinthandpleat.com with the article title, the specific error, and a source if possible. We will review and update promptly.
