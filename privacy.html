Privacy Policy

We believe your data is yours. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you can control it — in plain language.

Last updated: 1 March 2025

1. Introduction

PLINTH & PLEAT (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates the website at plinthandpleat.com and its related newsletters and services. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information with transparency and care.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and share information about you when you visit our website, subscribe to our newsletter, submit a contact form, or interact with us in other ways.

By using our site, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the site.

2. Information we collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide and improve our services. This falls into three categories:

Information you provide directly

  • Contact form: name, email address, subject, and message content.
  • Newsletter signup: email address.
  • Correspondence: any information you include when you email us directly.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data: pages viewed, time on page, referring URLs, and general interaction with the site.
  • Device and browser data: IP address, browser type, operating system, and screen resolution.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: see Section 6 below for details.

Information from third parties

We do not purchase or receive personal data from data brokers. If you sign up through a third-party platform (e.g., a newsletter provider), that platform may share your email address with us solely for the purpose of delivering our communications.

3. How we use your information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries, pitches, and messages.
  • To send our newsletter if you have opted in.
  • To analyse how the site is used so we can improve content and user experience.
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues or misuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Legal basis for processing (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, our legal basis for processing your personal data is:

  • Consent: for newsletter subscriptions and non-essential cookies. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interest: for responding to inquiries, improving our site, and ensuring security.
  • Legal obligation: where we are required to retain or disclose information by law.

5. Sharing your information

We share your information only in limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: trusted third parties who help us operate the site and deliver emails (e.g., email delivery platforms, analytics providers). These providers are bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal requirements: if required by law, regulation, or valid legal process.
  • Business transfers: in the unlikely event of a merger or acquisition, your data may be transferred as part of the business assets, with notice to you.

6. Cookies and tracking

We use minimal cookies and similar technologies. Essential cookies are necessary for the site to function. Analytics cookies help us understand which articles are read and how visitors navigate the site — this data is aggregated and anonymised where possible.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some site functionality. For more details, see our Cookies Policy.

7. Data retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy:

  • Contact form submissions: retained for up to 12 months after the inquiry is resolved, unless a longer period is required for legal reasons.
  • Newsletter subscriptions: retained until you unsubscribe, plus a short period for suppression purposes.
  • Analytics data: retained in aggregate form for up to 26 months.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure: request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Restriction: request that we limit processing of your data.
  • Portability: request transfer of your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection: object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent: where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@plinthandpleat.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. These include encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls, and regular reviews of our data practices.

However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's privacy

Our site is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

11. International transfers

Our servers and service providers may be located outside your country of residence. Where we transfer personal data internationally, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as standard contractual clauses approved by relevant authorities.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will post a notice on this page and update the “Last updated” date at the top. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

13. Contact us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact:
